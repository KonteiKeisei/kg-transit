Airline liveries are not shipped: a GM can use their own through the **Plane liveries folder**
setting, from sources they have the right to use. Without one, a plane's tail is painted in its
airline's colour.

Airline names and colours appear as part of the 1986 setting. The airlines and their marks
belong to their owners; KG Transit is not affiliated with any of them.

The build tools (`tools/`, not shipped) used the **Overpass API** public servers
(overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, maps.mail.ru) for the networks and airports, and the
**Wikidata Query Service** for opening years.

Requires **Calendaria** (https://github.com/Sayshal/calendaria) for its clock, weather and
time-skip cinematic, used through its public API. Works with the **dnd5e** system's coins and
party actors, and with **[KG Cities](https://github.com/KonteiKeisei/kg-cities)** for real city scenes.
